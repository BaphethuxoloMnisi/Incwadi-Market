# Privacy Policy for Incwadi Market

Last Updated: June 2026

Incwadi Market is a mobile application that allows students to buy and sell textbooks.

## Information We Collect

The application may collect and store user information provided during registration, including:

* Username
* Email address
* Login credentials

This information is stored locally on the user's device using Android SharedPreferences.

## How Information Is Used

The information is used to:

* Authenticate users
* Maintain user sessions
* Provide access to application features

## Data Sharing

Incwadi Market does not sell, share, or transfer user information to third parties.

## Data Storage

User information is stored locally on the device. No cloud database is used by the application.

## Account Deletion

Users may delete their account information by clearing the application data or uninstalling the application.
